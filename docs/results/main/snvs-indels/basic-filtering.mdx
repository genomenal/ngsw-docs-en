# Basic Filtering of Variants

Basic filtering columns are at the top of the table:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/basic_filtering.png').default} width="900"/>
</p>

:::info
On ["Profile settings" page](/settings/profile#filtering-mode), you can configure which filtering

## Basic Filters {#basic-filtering-columns}

:::info
On ["Profile settings" page](/settings/profile#filtering-mode), you can change the choice of basic filters.
The following describes in detail those filters that are chosen by default.
:::

:::note
Table filtering is saved when you exit the SNV Viewer page.
:::

### 1. Origin {#origin}

Filtering by mutation type: Somatic, Germline.

:::note
"Origin" filter column is hidden in the Basic Filtering mode
of [SNV Viewer with germline mutations](/results/main/snvs-indels/variant-viewer).
:::

Both here and in all subsequent cases, <b><i>the filtering algorithm</i></b> is as follows:
- Click on the filter field;
- Select the needed filtering option from the drop-down list, or type the option you are looking for in the
search box and select from the found.

Done! Filter applied. The selected filter can be seen in the field (in this case, the "Somatic" origin is selected):

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/set_option.png').default} width="150"/>
</p>

### 2. Gene panel {#gene-panel}

Filtering variants by localization in certain genes from the panel.
You can add a gene panel <a href="/settings/gene-panels">on "Gene Panels" page</a> or import a panel
from the Library of the most common clinical panels. To import, click on "<i>Import from Library</i>":

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/gene_panel.png').default} width="300"/>
</p>

Select the required panels by ticking them in the window that opens:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/gene_panels/checked_panels.png').default} width="600"/>
</p>

and click on <img src={require('/img/gene_panels/import_btn.png').default} width="150"/> to add panel.

### 3. Gene {#gene}

Filtering variants by localization in the gene transcript. The transcript is given in brackets next to
the official gene name. You can filter variants by several genes at once. To do this, tick the required genes
and click outside the value area:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/filter_by_gene.png').default} width="200"/>
</p>

If several transcripts are known for a gene, the list presents all of them:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/many_transcripts.png').default} height="400"/>
</p>

### 4. Ontology {#hpo}

Filter variants by their localization in genes associated with terms
from [the Human Phenotype Ontology (HPO)](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols/ontologies/hp)
and [the Mondo Disease Ontology (Mondo)](https://mondo.monarchinitiative.org/). Click the ontology filter
field. The search dialog that opens displays the main phenotype groups in a drop-down list:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/hpo_search.png').default} width="800"/>
</p>

**Methods for searching and selecting a phenotype or disease for filtering:**
- **Search directly in the drop-down list.** You can navigate the phenotype groups using
the <img src={require('/img/vv/show.png').default} height="15"/> and <img src={require('/img/vv/hide.png').default} height="10"/> buttons
to expand and collapse the list of terms within each group, respectively:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/list_of_phenotypes.png').default} width="270"/>
</p>

You can also search for a phenotype or disease:
- by its full or partial name;
- by its HPO ID (for example, HP:0009733 or 0009733);
- by its Mondo ID (for example, MONDO:0009733 or 0009733).

For example, searching for "glioma" returns several matching terms:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/glioma_search.png').default} width="800"/>
</p>

To select a term for filtering, click the corresponding row. You can filter by either an entire phenotype
group or an individual phenotype. After selecting a phenotype (for example, Glioma), its information card
is displayed:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/glioma_page.png').default} width="800"/>
</p>

The left side of the card displays the full phenotype or disease name with a link to the corresponding page
in the HPO or Mondo ontology (provided by [the Monarch Initiative](https://monarchinitiative.org/)), as well as
all available information from the selected ontology, including the description, synonyms, comment,
and PubMed references.

The center of the card displays the term hierarchy. Parent terms of the selected disease (in this example,
Glioma) are shown above it, indicating the phenotype groups to which it belongs. Child terms (phenotypes and
diseases that belong to the Glioma group) are displayed below.

The right side of the card contains a list of genes associated with the selected phenotype or disease.

Above the phenotype card, the terms selected for filtering are displayed together with their ontology
identifiers. The number of genes associated with each term is shown in parentheses. To open a phenotype
card, click the corresponding term. To remove a term from the filter,
click the <img src={require('/img/vv/delete_term_btn.png').default} width="15"/> button:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/delete_term.png').default} width="700"/>
</p>

- **Search using the term hierarchy.** The phenotype card allows you to navigate through the ontology
hierarchy. Click the name of a parent or child phenotype to open its card.<br></br>
If the term has not yet been selected for filtering and associated genes are available,
click <img src={require('/img/vv/add_term.png').default} width="150"/> to add it to the variant filter.

- **Search in multi-select mode.** Click the right button of the search mode switcher in the upper-left
corner of the dialog: <img src={require('/img/vv/switch_mode.png').default} width="120"/>. A list of the
main phenotype groups with selection checkboxes is displayed.

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/multiselect_mode_hpo.png').default} width="800"/>
</p>

Search can be performed:
- by full or partial name;
- by HPO ID (for example, HP:0009733 or 0009733);
- by Mondo ID (for example, MONDO:0009733 or 0009733).

For example, searching for "glioma" returns several matching terms:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/multiselect_hpo.png').default} width="800"/>
</p>

In multi-select mode, you can select multiple terms simultaneously from the drop-down list. To include
a phenotype in the filter, select its corresponding checkbox.

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/glioma_checked.png').default} width="400"/>
</p>

When you have finished selecting phenotypes, click anywhere outside the drop-down list but within the
search dialog. The selected filter terms are displayed at the top of the dialog. The number of genes
associated with each phenotype is shown in parentheses. Click a phenotype to open its information card.
To remove a phenotype, click the <img src={require('/img/vv/delete_term_btn.png').default} width="15"/> button.

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/multiselect_mode.png').default} width="800"/>
</p>

To ***filter variants*** by genes associated with the selected phenotypes,
click <img src={require('/img/custom-annot/ok_btn.png').default} width="130"/>.
To view the list of selected terms, hover over the ontology filter field:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/chosen_phenotypes.png').default} width="400"/>
</p>

To ***clear filtering*** for all selected terms, hover over the ontology filter field and click the cross
icon that appears:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/cancel_hpo.png').default} width="150"/>
</p>

Alternatively, click the ontology filter field and modify the selection in the phenotype search dialog.

### 5. Min depth level {#depth-scale}

Filtering by sequencing depth.
You can vary the level using both the plus/minus and the slider.
Between 1x and 50x the filter step (scale) is 1, between 50x and 100x is 3, and between 100x and 300x is 7.

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/seq_depth.png').default} width="350"/>
</p>

## Reset Filtering {#reset-filtering}

To clear ***all*** filters, click on <img src={require('/img/vv/clear_filters.png').default} width="40"/>.

To reset ***a separate filter***:
- Click on the filter field and then on <img src={require('/img/vv/clear_option.png').default} width="70"/>.
- If the filter allows to select several options at once (for example, filter by Gene), then you can remove
one of the selected filter options. To do this, click on the filter field and uncheck the option.
- To change the selected phenotypes used for variant filtering, click the ontology filter field and modify
the selection in the phenotype search dialog.
To clear filtering for all selected terms, hover over the ontology filter field and click the cross icon that appears:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/cancel_hpo.png').default} width="150"/>
</p>
