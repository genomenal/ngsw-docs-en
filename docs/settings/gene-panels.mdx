# Gene Panels

In this section, you can add gene panels so that you can filter the discovered variants by them.
The gene panel is a set of genes of interest (for example, genes associated with a specified phenotype).

To open the section with gene panels, go to the "**Gene panels**" page from the "Settings" page block
through the navigation panel on the left:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/gene_panels/gene_panels_page.png').default} width="900"/>
</p>

## Add a gene panel {#add-gene-panel}

There are four ways to add a panel to the system:
1. [Selection of genes of interest from all genes (by name or Ensembl code)](/settings/gene-panels#add-from-all-genes-list);
2. [Adding genes associated with a certain phenotype](/settings/gene-panels#add-from-hpo);
3. [Import of a pre-prepared gene list](/settings/gene-panels#import-gene-list);
4. [Import panel from the library of the most common clinical panels](/settings/gene-panels#import-from-library).

:::tip
When adding a panel, the first and second ways can be combined.
:::

### 1. Adding genes from the list of all genes to the panel {#add-from-all-genes-list}

1. Click on <img src={require('/img/eng/add_gene_panel.png').default} width="170"/> to open the form of
adding a gene panel:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/gene_panels/add_gene_panel_form.png').default} height="700"/>
</p>

2. In the opened form, enter gene panel name in the **Name** field.
You can also add a panel description.

3. Add genes to the panel. To do this, enter official gene symbol or Ensembl gene ID (ENSG...) into the search box.
Among the found genes, select one or several ones, click on its/their row(s), and then
click on <img src={require('/img/gene_panels/add_btn.png').default} width="40"/>:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/gene_panels/add_gene.png').default} height="350"/>
</p>

Among the genes added to the panel, you can search, as well as remove genes from the panel (to do so, click on the
gene row, and then click on <img src={require('/img/gene_panels/remove_gene.png').default} width="40"/>):

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/gene_panels/remove_example.png').default} height="350"/>
</p>

4. After you have added all the needed genes to the panel,
click on <img src={require('/img/eng/create_btn.png').default} width="130"/>.
A gene panel will be added to the system.

### 2. Adding genes from ontology to the panel {#add-from-hpo}

1. Click on <img src={require('/img/eng/add_gene_panel.png').default} width="170"/> to open the form of
adding a gene panel:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/gene_panels/add_gene_panel_form.png').default} height="700"/>
</p>

2. In the opened form, enter gene panel name in the **Name** field.
You can also add a panel description.

3. Click on <img src={require('/img/gene_panels/add_from_hpo.png').default} width="280"/>.
You will see the search dialog to find terms
from [the Human Phenotype Ontology (HPO)](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols/ontologies/hp)
and [the Mondo Disease Ontology (Mondo)](https://mondo.monarchinitiative.org/).
The dialog displays the main phenotype groups in a drop-down list:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/hpo_search.png').default} width="800"/>
</p>

4. Search and select phenotypes in one of the following ways:
- **Search directly in the drop-down list.** You can navigate the phenotype groups
using the <img src={require('/img/vv/show.png').default} height="15"/> and <img src={require('/img/vv/hide.png').default} height="10"/> buttons
to expand and collapse the list of terms within each group, respectively:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/list_of_phenotypes.png').default} width="270"/>
</p>

You can also search for a phenotype or disease:
- by its full or partial name;
- by its HPO ID (for example, HP:0009733 or 0009733);
- by its Mondo ID (for example, MONDO:0009733 or 0009733).

For example, searching for "glioma" returns several matching terms:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/glioma_search.png').default} width="800"/>
</p>

To select a term, click the corresponding row. You can select either an entire phenotype
group or an individual phenotype. After selecting a phenotype (for example, Glioma), its information card
is displayed:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/glioma_page.png').default} width="800"/>
</p>

The left side of the card displays the full phenotype or disease name with a link to the corresponding page
in the HPO or Mondo ontology (provided by [the Monarch Initiative](https://monarchinitiative.org/)), as well as
all available information from the selected ontology, including the description, synonyms, comment,
and PubMed references.

The center of the card displays the term hierarchy. Parent terms of the selected disease (in this example,
Glioma) are shown above it, indicating the phenotype groups to which it belongs. Child terms (phenotypes and
diseases that belong to the Glioma group) are displayed below.

The right side of the card contains a list of genes associated with the selected phenotype or disease.
These genes will be included in the gene panel when selecting a term.

Above the phenotype card, the selected terms are displayed together with their ontology
identifiers. The number of genes associated with each term is shown in parentheses. To open a phenotype
card, click the corresponding term. To remove a term,
click the <img src={require('/img/vv/delete_term_btn.png').default} width="15"/> button:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/delete_term.png').default} width="700"/>
</p>

- **Search using the term hierarchy.** The phenotype card allows you to navigate through the ontology
hierarchy. Click the name of a parent or child phenotype to open its card.<br></br>
If the term has not yet been selected and associated genes are available,
click <img src={require('/img/vv/add_term.png').default} width="150"/> to include those genes in the panel.

- **Search in multi-select mode.** Click the right button of the search mode switcher in the upper-left
corner of the dialog: <img src={require('/img/vv/switch_mode.png').default} width="120"/>. A list of the
main phenotype groups with selection checkboxes is displayed.

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/multiselect_mode_hpo.png').default} width="800"/>
</p>

Search can be performed:
- by full or partial name;
- by HPO ID (for example, HP:0009733 or 0009733);
- by Mondo ID (for example, MONDO:0009733 or 0009733).

For example, searching for "glioma" returns several matching terms:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/multiselect_hpo.png').default} width="800"/>
</p>

In multi-select mode, you can select multiple terms simultaneously from the drop-down list. To select
a phenotype, activate its corresponding checkbox.

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/glioma_checked.png').default} width="400"/>
</p>

When you have finished selecting phenotypes, click anywhere outside the drop-down list but within the
search dialog. The selected filter terms are displayed at the top of the dialog. The number of genes
associated with each phenotype is shown in parentheses. Click a phenotype to open its information card.
To remove a phenotype, click the <img src={require('/img/vv/delete_term_btn.png').default} width="15"/> button.

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/vv/multiselect_mode.png').default} width="800"/>
</p>

To close the phenotype search dialog without adding ontology-associated genes to the panel,
click <img src={require('/img/gene_panels/cancel_btn.png').default} width="150"/> or anywhere outside the dialog.

To add genes associated with the selected phenotypes to the panel,
click <img src={require('/img/custom-annot/ok_btn.png').default} width="130"/>.

The added genes are displayed in the list on the right. You can search within the list or remove genes
from the panel. To remove a gene, select it in the list and then
click <img src={require('/img/gene_panels/remove_gene.png').default} width="40"/>:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/gene_panels/remove_hpo_gene.png').default} height="350"/>
</p>

5. After you have added all the needed genes to the panel,
click on <img src={require('/img/eng/create_btn.png').default} width="130"/>.
A gene panel will be added to the system.

### 3. Import gene list {#import-gene-list}

1. Click on <img src={require('/img/eng/import_gene_list.png').default} width="170"/> to open the form of
importing gene list:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/gene_panels/import_form.png').default} height="500"/>
</p>

2. In the opened form, enter gene panel name in the **Name** field.
You can also add a panel description.

3. Enter gene list in the **Genes** area. The list must include only official gene symbols and/or
Ensembl gene IDs (ENSG...) separated by commas or new lines.
It is preferable to use Ensembl ID or unique gene synonym that correspond to only one Ensembl ID,
since when using gene symbols, ambiguity may arise due to the existence of human alternative sequence
genes (in the given example, the *MUC6* gene has three such genes:
[ENSG00000277281](https://www.ensembl.org/Homo_sapiens/Gene/Summary?db=core;g=ENSG00000277281),
[ENSG00000277518](https://www.ensembl.org/Homo_sapiens/Gene/Summary?db=core;g=ENSG00000277518),
[ENSG00000283350](https://www.ensembl.org/Homo_sapiens/Gene/Summary?db=core;g=ENSG00000283350)):

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/gene_panels/ambigous_gene.png').default} width="600"/>
</p>

4. Click on <img src={require('/img/eng/create_btn.png').default} width="130"/> to add gene panel to the system.

### 4. Import gene panel from library {#import-from-library}

1. Click on <img src={require('/img/eng/import_from_library.png').default} width="170"/> to open the form of
importing gene panel:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/gene_panels/import_from_library.png').default} height="700"/>
</p>

2. In the opened form, tick the required panels:

<p align="center">
<img src={require('/img/gene_panels/checked_panels.png').default} width="600"/>
</p>

3. Click on <img src={require('/img/gene_panels/import_btn.png').default} width="170"/>.

## Edit a gene panel {#edit-panel}

To add or remove genes from the added panel, click on the panel row on the "Gene panels" page.
In the opened form, you can change the name and description of the panel,
remove and add genes to the panel (you can add genes either from the list of all genes or from ontology).
After making changes click on <img src={require('/img/group_analysis/save_btn.png').default} width="150"/>.

## Delete a gene panel {#delete-panel}

To remove a gene panel from the system, hover over its row on the "Gene panels" page and click on the
appeared <img src={require('/img/eng/delete_btn.png').default} width="21"/> button.
